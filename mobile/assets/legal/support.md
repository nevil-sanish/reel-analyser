# Support and Refund Policy

Reel Analyser · Effective 11 November 2026 · Version 1.0

Please read this with the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy.

## 1. The app is free

Reel Analyser is free and will always stay free to use. There are no ads, no paid features, no subscriptions and nothing locked behind a payment. Every feature works the same whether or not you support the app.

## 2. What support is

Support is a voluntary gift to the developer, Nevil Mathew Sanish. It helps him keep improving this app and build more apps like it in the future.

- **Nothing is sold.** A support payment does not buy a product, a service, a feature, priority help or any other benefit, and it creates no contract to deliver anything.
- **Not a charity.** Reel Analyser is an individual's project, not a registered charity or non-profit, so a support payment is not tax-deductible.
- **Your own money.** Only support with money that is yours to give. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian first.
- **Never required.** Nobody, including the developer, will ever ask you to pay to use, unlock or keep the app. Any message that says otherwise is not from us.

## 3. How you can support

- **Buy Me a Coffee** (buymeacoffee.com/nevilsanish): the payment is handled by Buy Me a Coffee and its payment processors (such as Stripe), under their own terms and privacy policy. Your card details go to them, never to the developer or the app.
- **UPI** (UPI ID nevilsanish@oksbi): the payment is made from your own UPI app and bank, under their terms and the rules of the UPI network. The app only opens your UPI app with the developer's UPI ID filled in. You choose the amount and confirm the payment there. The app never sees your bank details, PIN or the payment.

The app itself does not take, process or store any payment.

## 4. Refunds

You can have your support refunded, for any reason, if you ask within 7 days of the payment.

- **How to ask:** email nevilsanish@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment with the subject "Refund", and include the details of the transaction: the date and time, the amount, how you paid (Buy Me a Coffee or UPI), the transaction or UTR reference, and the name, email or UPI ID you paid from.
- **What happens next:** once the payment is found and matched, the full amount you paid is refunded to the same method you paid with (the same UPI ID or bank account, or through Buy Me a Coffee to the same card). We aim to send the refund within 7 working days of matching the payment. Your bank or payment app may take a few more days to show it.
- **After 7 days**, support is final and is not refunded, except where the law requires a refund.
- **Mistakes:** if you paid twice, paid the wrong amount or paid by mistake, tell us the same way. A clear mistake is refunded even if you notice it a little after 7 days.
- **Unauthorised payments:** if someone paid without permission (for example a child, or a stolen card or account), contact your bank or payment app as well as us.

Please write to us before you open a dispute or chargeback with your bank, so we can refund you directly and quickly.

## 5. Your information

When you support the app, the payment service shows the developer what it shows any payee: usually your name or UPI ID, the amount, the date, a transaction reference and any message you add. Buy Me a Coffee may also share your email address. This is used only to keep a record of the payment, to process refunds and as tax and accounting law requires. It is never added to the app, sold or shared for marketing. See the Privacy Policy, section 5.

## 6. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version appears in the app with a new date. A change never removes a refund you have already asked for.
