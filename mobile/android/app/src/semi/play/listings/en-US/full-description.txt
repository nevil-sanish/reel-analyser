Share a reel, a YouTube Short, a public LinkedIn post or a picture to Reel Analyser, and it becomes a searchable note: a title, a summary, key points, tags and the transcript. Then ask questions about everything you saved.

<b>Notes and folders</b>
<ul>
<li>Notes from Instagram reels and posts, YouTube Shorts, public LinkedIn posts (text, pictures or video) and shared pictures</li>
<li>Your own folders. New notes go into the matching folder on save, and Organize sorts everything with your AI key, suggesting new folders only for real topics</li>
<li>Chat with all your notes, with one note, or with one folder</li>
<li>Share a note with its link, or share a chat</li>
<li>Archive, search, export and import, light and dark themes</li>
</ul>

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no servers of our own</li>
<li>Notes stay on your phone. A reel goes straight from your phone to the AI service you choose (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude or Groq), with your own API key</li>
<li>LinkedIn and Instagram pages are read like a browser would, with no login</li>
</ul>

<b>Semi build</b>
This is the build that asks Android for nothing but the internet: no Accessibility, no Usage access, no notifications. It has every notes feature. For reel counting, the reel blocker and daily goals, install Reel Analyser (the full build) instead. Both can be installed side by side.

The app is free and always will be. Supporting the developer is optional, from the About screen.

Reel Analyser is not affiliated with Instagram, Meta, YouTube, Google, LinkedIn or any AI provider. It needs your own API key for an AI service (Gemini has a free tier).
